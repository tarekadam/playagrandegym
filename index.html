A Different Kind of Gym

A small, private strength training space surrounded by the jungle.

With just 5 or 6 people training at a time, you'll enjoy a relaxed atmosphere, regular reserved workout times, and the freedom to follow your own routine.

No crowds. No waiting for equipment. Just a great place to get stronger.

Women's Strength Club

Small-group strength training, three days a week, in structured six-week cycles. Groups are limited to six women.

Open Gym

Train at your own pace in our private strength gym, with regular reserved workout times, flexible scheduling, and a maximum of six people per session.
